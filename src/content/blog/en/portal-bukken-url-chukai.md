## Can I buy a property I found on SUUMO through a different agency?

**For most second-hand properties, yes.** Send us the listing link, and we act as your buyer’s agent from the viewing through to the contract and closing. If you come to us through this website, our brokerage fee is half the legal maximum.

Japanese property portals such as SUUMO, LIFULL HOME'S and at home show an agency to contact for each listing, so it is easy to assume you have to go through that agency. In fact, most second-hand condominiums and houses can be bought on the same terms through another agency.

## Why can another agency submit an offer?

**An agency holding an exclusive mandate (senin or senzoku senin) must register the property on REINS, which shares the listing with other agencies.** REINS is the property database for licensed agencies, run by the real estate information network organizations designated by the Minister of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism.

- **Exclusive mandates (senin / senzoku senin)**: The seller entrusts the sale to one agency only. That agency must register the property on REINS within a set period (Real Estate Brokerage Act).
- **General mandates (ippan)**: The seller entrusts the sale to several agencies. Registration on REINS is not required, though many of these properties are registered anyway.

Any licensed agency can submit an offer as the buyer’s agent on a property registered on REINS. Since January 2025, the status of properties under these two types of mandate, such as “available”, “written offer received” or “temporarily withheld at the seller’s request”, must also be recorded on REINS (details of the rule to be confirmed). The aim is to stop listing agencies from keeping properties to themselves (“kakoikomi”).

These rates apply to new inquiries received through this website, LINE or a personal referral. For properties we list ourselves on behalf of a seller (including on portal sites), the fee stated in that listing and in the brokerage agreement applies (within the legal maximum). Properties priced at ¥8 million or less (excluding tax) are not eligible either (a government notice provides a special rule for this price band, so we explain the fee before the brokerage agreement).

## What happens after I send the link?

**We check the property and tell you whether we can handle it, then move on to the viewing, offer and contract.**

1. **Send the link**: Paste the listing URL into the “Link to a property you are interested in” field of the inquiry form, or send it on LINE. Several links are fine.
2. **We check**: We check the REINS registration and ask the listing agency whether the property is still available and whether we can introduce a buyer. We also give you the management and repair-reserve fees and a comparison with past sale prices.
3. **Viewing**: We arrange a time with the listing agency and meet you on site.
4. **Offer and negotiation**: We negotiate the price, handover date and other terms on your side.
5. **Contract and closing**: We take you through the explanation of important matters, the sale contract, your mortgage, and closing and handover. No office visit is needed; paperwork is handled via form, LINE and online meetings.

## Are there properties you cannot handle?

**Properties not on REINS, and those whose listing agency does not accept other agencies, may not be possible.** Properties owned by a real estate company can sometimes be handled if they are on REINS.

- **Properties not on REINS**: General-mandate properties that have not been registered, and new condominiums sold at a developer’s showroom, for example.
- **Properties not open to other agencies**: Properties where the listing agency is looking for a buyer itself. In that case we tell you exactly what we found.
- **Properties already under offer**: Introductions may be paused because someone else’s offer came first.

We tell you whether we can handle a property within 3 business days of receiving the link.

## What if I have already contacted the listing agency?

**If you have only made an inquiry, you can usually still ask us. But as a rule we cannot take over a property you have already viewed through that agency or made an offer on.**

When you use a portal’s inquiry button, the listing agency starts acting as your buyer’s agent as well. If you switch to another agency after a viewing arranged by them, or after submitting an offer, it can lead to a dispute over which agency brokered the deal. For those properties, please continue with the first agency.

When you find a property you like, **send us the link before contacting the listing agency**.

## Does the same work for rentals?

**Yes.** Send us the link of a rental listing and we will check whether we can apply on your behalf. Our fee is half a month’s rent (0.5 months plus tax). For a ¥150,000-a-month apartment, that is ¥82,500 less than an agency charging one month plus tax (¥165,000).

Some rental properties, such as those a management company only lets itself, cannot be applied for through other agencies. We will tell you if that is the case.
