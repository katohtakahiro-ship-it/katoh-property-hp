## What kind of company is Katoh Property Management?

**In short: a family-run, licensed real estate brokerage based in Musashikosugi that handles rentals and sales across Kawasaki, Yokohama and the greater Tokyo area.** Brokerage fees are half the standard rate for both rentals and sales, and everything is handled online via form or LINE, with no office visit needed.

Hello, I am Takahiro Katoh, a managing member of Katoh Property Management LLC. For the first article on this blog, let me introduce the company and myself.

We are a small company run by my family: my parents, my wife and me. My parents have run a separate real estate company in Yokohama and Kawasaki for more than 40 years. This company is my way of carrying that experience and local knowledge forward in a new form.

- **Company**: Katoh Property Management LLC (合同会社加藤プロパティマネジメント)
- **License**: Real estate broker, Kanagawa Prefecture Governor (2) No. 30531
- **Address**: 4-51-1 Kizuki, Nakahara-ku, Kawasaki, Kanagawa 211-0025, Japan
- **Managing members**: Yoshie Katoh, Takahiro Katoh (family-run)
- **Service area**: Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and Saitama. Home ground: Musashikosugi, Kawasaki and Yokohama
- **Brokerage fees**: rentals, half a month's rent (0.5 months plus tax); sales, half the legal maximum (1.5% of the sale price plus ¥30,000, plus tax)

For the Tokyu Toyoko line neighborhoods of Musashikosugi, Motosumiyoshi and Shinmaruko, and for the tower condominiums of Kawasaki and Yokohama, I can speak from the perspective of someone who has actually lived here.

## How can the brokerage fee be half price?

**In short: we have no shopfront. The office is home-based and the whole process runs online, which keeps fixed costs and advertising costs low.**

A typical agency pays for a shop near the station, shop staff and listings on portal sites, and recovers those costs through brokerage fees. We have none of them. Consultations come in by form and LINE, interviews and contracts are handled online, and viewings are arranged on site. That structure is what makes a half-price fee workable.

The numbers are the same everywhere on this site.

| Transaction | Typical fee | Katoh Property Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rental | One month's rent plus tax | Half a month's rent (0.5 months plus tax) |
| Sale | Legal maximum: 3% of the sale price + ¥60,000 plus tax | Half the legal maximum: 1.5% + ¥30,000 plus tax |

A fee is charged only when a contract is concluded. Nothing is charged for consultations or estimates.

## Which areas and which transactions?

**In short: Musashikosugi, Kawasaki and Yokohama are our home ground, and we handle both rentals and sales anywhere in the greater Tokyo area.**

- **Rentals (tenant side)**: from the search and viewings to the lease contract, across Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and Saitama
- **Sales**: buying and selling land, condominiums and houses, across Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba and Saitama
- **Landlord listings (tenant recruitment)**: we take on properties from landlords **only in Nakahara-ku, Saiwai-ku and Kawasaki-ku in Kawasaki City**. Keeping this close to home lets us handle viewings and management properly
- **Areas**: Kanagawa (Musashikosugi, Kawasaki, Yokohama) first, plus Tokyo, Chiba and Saitama

For the tower condominiums of Kawasaki and Yokohama, this blog will publish a quarterly review of transaction prices based on the public data released by Japan's Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism, with sources cited.

## How does the process work?

**In short: contact us by form or LINE, then an interview, property proposals, viewings on site, and the contract. No office visit needed.**

1. **Contact**: send a message through the form on this site or via LINE
2. **Interview**: area, budget, timing and conditions, discussed online
3. **Proposals**: we shortlist several properties that match
4. **Viewings**: we meet you on site and visit the properties together
5. **Contract and handover**: half-price brokerage fee for rentals and sales

We work in English as well as Japanese. In Japan the Japanese contract and the Japanese explanation of important matters are the legally binding documents. We provide English translations for reference and explain every document in English. Please confirm the legal treatment for your own transaction before signing.
